About

What is Diaspora Nearby?

Diaspora Nearby is a mobile-first platform connecting diaspora communities worldwide. $DIASPORA is a community-owned ERC-20 utility token deployed on Coinbase L2 Base Blockchain. It powers decentralized governance, treasury management, and token generation events (TGEs) across a 10-epoch lifecycle designed to grow with the global diaspora population.

Community-First

No ICO or public sale. $DIASPORA is primarily earned through Proof of Action (PoA) — referrals, engagement, governance participation, and community building. A SAFT is available to all eligible investors under Reg CF.

Mint-and-Lock

Each epoch's token supply is pre-minted and locked on-chain in the TGE Controller. Tokens are only distributed via transparent TGE events, verifiable on Basescan.

Deflationary by Design

10% of all DSP expansion is permanently burned, creating deflationary pressure that increases scarcity as the ecosystem grows.

Proof of Community Trust

PoCT — a reputation score based on verifications, vouches, connections, and account age — determines your TGE conversion efficiency.

Community Treasuries

The Engine of Localized Economic Sovereignty

Every DSP-earning activity automatically funds a hierarchical network of community treasuries — from global, down through continents, countries, states, and cities. These treasuries accumulate real economic value that communities govern collectively, creating self-funding diaspora institutions at every geographic level.

Automatic Allocation

When a user earns DSP, 10% of the expansion is automatically split across up to 7 community treasuries based on their home and host locations. No manual transfers needed.

Geographic Hierarchy

Each user funds both home-country and host-country treasuries. A Kenyan in Canada simultaneously funds treasuries in Kenya and Canada — from continent down to city level.

Community Governance

Each community governs its treasury. Members vote on proposals for how funds are allocated — grants, infrastructure, events, and Diaspora Direct Investments lending pools.

APoCT TGE Efficiency

Community treasuries convert at the Average PoCT (APoCT) of their members during TGE events. The higher the collective trust of a community, the more $DIASPORA its treasury yields using the same decile scale as individuals.

Diaspora Direct Investments

Community treasuries aren't just savings — they're active capital. Members can apply for micro-loans directly from their local treasury, funded by the accumulated $DIASPORA. If a local treasury doesn't have sufficient funds, loan requests cascade upward through the hierarchy — city → state → country → continent → global — until a treasury with adequate funds is found. This creates a self-sustaining lending network that operates without traditional banks or intermediaries.

Community treasuries convert at APoCT efficiency — the weighted average PoCT of all members, applied on the same decile scale APoCT

How It Works

From DSP to $DIASPORA

1. Earn DSP

Complete activities: refer friends, engage with communities, explore the app, build trust through PoCT.

2. Qualify for TGE

Accumulate DSP to reach the epoch's TGE threshold. Your PoCT score determines your conversion rate.

3. Token Generation

At each TGE, qualified users receive $DIASPORA tokens from the locked on-chain supply, proportional to DSP × efficiency.

4. On-Chain Ownership

$DIASPORA tokens are real ERC-20 assets on Coinbase L2 Base Blockchain. Fully verifiable, transferable, and community-governed.

Security

Security & Transparency

UUPS Proxy Pattern

Contracts are upgradeable via UUPS proxies with OpenZeppelin's battle-tested libraries, allowing bug fixes while preserving state.

Role-Based Access

Admin, Minter, Upgrader, and TGE Oracle roles are separated. No single key controls everything.

On-Chain Verification

All minting, locking, and distribution events are recorded on-chain. Anyone can verify on the blockchain.

Immutable Burns

Burned tokens are sent to the zero address — permanently removed from circulation, verifiable on-chain.
