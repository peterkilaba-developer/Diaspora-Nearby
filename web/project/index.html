About

What is Diaspora Nearby?

Diaspora Nearby is a mobile-first platform connecting diaspora communities worldwide. $DIASPORA is a community-owned ERC-20 utility token deployed on Ethereum Base Blockchain. It powers decentralized governance, treasury management, and token generation events (TGEs) across a 10-epoch lifecycle designed to grow with the global diaspora population.

Community-First

No ICO or public sale. $DIASPORA is primarily earned through Proof of Action (P.o.S.A) — peer verification, engagement, governance participation, and community building. A SAFT is available to all eligible investors under Reg CF.

Mint-and-Lock

Each epoch's token supply is pre-minted and locked on-chain in the TGE Controller. Tokens are only distributed via transparent TDE releases, verifiable on Basescan.

Deflationary by Design

10% of all DSP expansion is permanently burned, creating deflationary pressure that increases scarcity as the ecosystem grows.

Proof of Community Trust

P.o.C.T — a trust score based on verifications, vouches, connections, and account age — determines your TDE distribution efficiency.

10-Epoch Lifecycle

$DIASPORA follows a 10-epoch growth model. Each epoch's supply is 10× the previous. Token supply is pre-minted and locked on-chain in the TGE Controller before distribution begins. Each epoch has one TGE and 10 TDE releases. TDEs occur at every 10% DSP-supply milestone inside the epoch, distributing the corresponding epoch decile based on users' accumulated DSP and P.o.C.T efficiency.

Community Treasuries

The Engine of Localized Economic Sovereignty

Every DSP-earning activity automatically funds a hierarchical network of community treasuries — from global, down through continents, countries, states, and cities. These treasuries accumulate real economic value that communities govern collectively, creating self-funding diaspora institutions at every geographic level.

Automatic Allocation

When a user earns DSP, 10% of the expansion is automatically split across up to 7 community treasuries based on their home and host locations. No manual transfers needed.

Geographic Hierarchy

Each user funds both home-country and host-country treasuries. A Kenyan in Canada simultaneously funds treasuries in Kenya and Canada — from continent down to city level.

Community Governance

Each community governs its treasury. Members who complete the 7 Genesis Milestones vote on proposals and community loans — with mandatory voting enforced (-1 P.C.T member / -10 P.C.T leader missed-vote penalty).

AP.C.T TDE Efficiency

Community treasuries convert at the Average P.C.T (AP.C.T) of their members during TDE releases. The higher the collective trust of a community, the more $DIASPORA its treasury yields using the same exact-percent efficiency rule as individuals.

Diaspora Direct Investments

Community treasuries aren't just savings — they're active capital. Members can apply for micro-loans directly from their local treasury, funded by the accumulated $DIASPORA. If a local treasury doesn't have sufficient funds, loan requests cascade upward through the hierarchy — city → state → country → continent → global — until a treasury with adequate funds is found. This creates a self-sustaining lending network that operates without traditional banks or intermediaries.

Community treasuries convert at AP.C.T efficiency — the weighted average P.C.T of all members, applied with the same no-trust, standard-trust, and full-trust rule AP.C.T

TGE Efficiency

P.C.T-Based Efficiency Scale

Your Proof of Community Trust (P.C.T) score determines what percentage of your DSP converts to $DIASPORA during a TDE release. Scores 1-10 convert at 0%, scores 11-90 convert at the exact P.C.T percentage, and scores 91-100 convert at 100%. Completing the 6 Base Genesis Milestones (Ubuntu Emergency Fund contribution deferred post-genesis) unlocks Genesis 7,000 $DIASPORA token distribution. Reaching 91+ P.C.T unlocks Peer Hosting, Peer Stay, Leadership Candidacy, and DDI Loan Eligibility. Standing below 91+ P.C.T revokes high-trust privileges without freezing TDE distributions (users participate at their efficiency decile). Users can claim earned tokens anytime via individual pull claims; only unearned tokens from the efficiency gate are burned to the dead address (0x000...dEaD).
