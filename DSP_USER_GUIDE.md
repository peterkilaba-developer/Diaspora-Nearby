# How to Earn $DIASPORA: A Guide to Social Capital

Welcome to Diaspora Nearby! Our platform rewards you not just for showing up, but for building genuine connections and trust within your community. Our economy is built on two core pillars: **Proof of Social Activities** and **Proof of Community Trust**. The token is always referred to as **$DIASPORA**.

## 1. Proof of Social Activities (P.o.S.A) - Earning DSP
**Proof of Social Activities** is simple: You earn off-chain **DSP** by fulfilling our mission: **Connecting with fellow Diaspora** and **keeping in touch with family and friends back home**. The more you interact, the more you earn. These DSP points are later converted to $DIASPORA at the TGE based on your Trust Score.

## 3. Proof of Community Trust (P.o.C.T) - Keeping Your $DIASPORA
Earning is only half the battle. **Proof of Community Trust** is how we ensure that $DIASPORA represents *real* value and *real* reputation.

### Earning P.o.C.T & The Decile Efficiency Gate
To prevent inflation and ensure active participation, your TGE conversion efficiency is tied to your Trust Score. 
*   **From the Beginning:** DSP and P.o.C.T are awarded instantly in real-time for all general activities (referrals, new chats, check-ins, creating events). You receive DSP + 1 P.o.C.T for each of these actions.
*   **Genesis Connection Milestones (The 10× Rule):** For the four Genesis connection milestones (Family, Friend, QR Exchange, and Vouched/Verified combined), you earn 1,000 DSP **instantly** per connection. However, the +10 P.o.C.T is awarded only at **10× intervals** (10, 100, 1,000).
*   **Profile Completion (The Genesis Exception):** The only milestone that does not pay out instantly is Profile Completion. It requires exactly 10 fields to fully complete, which then triggers a massive 10,000 DSP Genesis lump sum and +10 P.o.C.T all at once.

Your final Trust Score dictates your **Swap Rate** at the Token Generation Event.
*   *Formula*: `Efficiency = (Score / 10).ceil() / 10.0`
*   A user with a score of 95 gets 100% (1:1), while a user with a score of 51 gets only 60% conversion.

**In short: Be active to earn from the beginning. Hit 10-person Genesis Milestones to unlock massive one-time rewards.**

## 4. The 10,000 $DIASPORA Gate (TGE)
The final milestone is the **10,000 $DIASPORA Gate**. 
- **Proof of Commitment**: Once you reach 10,000 $DIASPORA and maintain a high Trust Score, you become eligible for the **Token Generation Event (TGE)**.
- **The Reset**: At the TGE, your activity is "crystallized." Your points will be swapped 1:1 for liquid tokens on the Coinbase L2 Base Blockchain, turning your social capital into real-world assets.

## 5. Frictionless Economy
All economic activities in Diaspora Nearby are **completely free of DSP fees**. You can:

*   **List and sell** on the Marketplace — no seller gate or listing fees
*   **Register a business** — no creation or product listing fees
*   **Create and attend events** — no event listing or attendance fees
*   **Host and travel** — hosting arrangements are fee-free

The DSP economy is sustained through **penalties** (see below), **protocol burns**, and **P.o.C.T efficiency** — not by taxing economic activity.
