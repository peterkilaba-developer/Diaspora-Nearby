# How to Earn DSP: A Guide to Social Capital

Welcome to Diaspora Nearby! Our platform rewards you not just for showing up, but for building genuine connections and trust within your community. Our economy is built on two core pillars: **Proof of Activity** and **Proof of Community Trust**.

## 1. Proof of Activity (PoA) - Earning DSP
**Proof of Activity** is simple: You earn DSP (Diaspora Social Points) by fulfilling our mission: **Connecting with fellow Diaspora** and **keeping in touch with family and friends back home**. The more you interact, the more you earn.

### Ways to Earn

**🚀 Growth & Starting Out**
*   **New User Bonus**: Earn **1,000 DSP** just for joining!
*   **Refer Family & Friends**: Earn **1,000 DSP** for every family member or friend who joins using your code.
*   **Complete Profile**: Earn **1,000 DSP** for fully setting up your profile.

**🤝 Building Connections**
*   **Start a New Conversation**: Earn **100 DSP** when you start a DM with a new person.
*   **Relationship Acceptance**: Earn **1,000 DSP** when you and another user agree to be "Connected".

**💬 Daily Engagement**
*   **Send Messages**: Earn **10 DSP** for every message you send to family or friends.
*   **Community Posts**: Earn **100 DSP** for posting in your local community (max 1/day).
*   **Daily Check-in**: Earn **100 DSP** just for opening the app daily.

**🌟 High-Impact Actions**
*   **Create an Event**: Earn **1,000 DSP** for organizing a local event.
*   **Create an Organization**: Earn **1,000 DSP** for listing your business or non-profit.
*   **Streaks**: Keep your activity up!
    *   **10-Day Streak**: **+1,000 DSP** bonus.
    *   **30-Day Streak**: **+3,000 DSP** bonus.

## 2. National Pride & The Leaderboard
Diaspora Nearby is not just an individual journey—it's a race for your nation.

*   **The 10% Collective Tribute**: Every time you and your fellow countrymen collectively earn 1,000,000 DSP, Diaspora Nearby mints an extra **100,000 DSP** and deposits it directly into your **Home Country Treasury**. 
*   **The Global Leaderboard**: Track your country's progress on the shared leaderboard. Compete with other nations to see who can build the largest treasury. Your individual activity directly drives your nation's rise to the top!
*   **Collective Proof of Trust (CPoCT)**: A high treasury balance demonstrates that your Diaspora community is the most active, trusted, and economically committed group on the planet.

---

## 3. Proof of Community Trust (PoCT) - Keeping Your DSP
Earning is only half the battle. **Proof of Community Trust** is how we ensure that DSP represents *real* value and *real* reputation.

### The Trust Slider
When you interact with others (in DMs or at Events), they can rate their trust in you using the **Trust Slider**:
*   **Red (Unverified)**: Suspicious or unknown.
*   **Yellow (Cautious)**: Tentative trust.
*   **Green (Verified)**: Full community trust.

### Why Trust Matters: The Decay System
To prevent inflation and ensure active participation, everyone's potential earnings naturally "decay" over time as your account gets older. 
*   **Without Trust**: Your rewards will shrink significantly over time (Decay).
*   **With Trust**: A high **Trust Score (Green)** acts as a shield. It **blocks the decay**, allowing you to keep earning full rewards even as a long-time user.

**In short: Be active to earn. Be trusted to keep earning at full speed.**

---

## 3. The 100,000 DSP Gate (TGE)
The final milestone is the **100,000 DSP Gate**. 
- **Proof of Commitment**: Once you reach 100,000 DSP and maintain a high Trust Score, you become eligible for the **Token Generation Event (TGE)**.
- **The Reset**: At the TGE, your activity is "crystallized." Your points will be swapped 1:1 for liquid tokens, turning your social capital into real-world assets.

## 4. The Justice System
We value quality over quantity.
*   **Spamming**: If you are reported for spam, you lose **100 DSP**.
*   **Rejection**: If you send a Relationship Request that is *rejected*, you lose **1,000 DSP**. (So only send requests to people you actually know or have connected with!)
