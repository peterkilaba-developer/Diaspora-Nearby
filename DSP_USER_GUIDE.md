# How to Earn $DIASPORA: A Guide to Social Capital

Welcome to Diaspora Nearby! Our platform rewards you not just for showing up, but for building genuine connections and trust within your community. Our economy is built on two core pillars: **Proof of Activity (P.O.A)** (earning DSP) and **Proof of Community Trust (PCT)** (earning $DIASPORA efficiency). The token is always referred to as **$DIASPORA**.

## 1. Proof of Activity (P.O.A) — Earning DSP
**Proof of Activity (P.O.A)** is simple: You earn off-chain **DSP** by fulfilling our mission: **Connecting with fellow Diaspora** and **keeping in touch with family and friends back home**. The more you interact, the more you earn. These DSP points are later converted to $DIASPORA at the TGE based on your Trust Score.

## 3. Proof of Community Trust (PCT) — Multiplier & Governance
Earning is only half the battle. **Proof of Community Trust (PCT)** is how we ensure that $DIASPORA represents *real* value, verified reputation, and civic governance.

### 🛡️ The Universal Prerequisite Gate (7 Genesis Milestones + 91+ PCT)
To unlock high-trust privileges, a member **must complete all 7 Genesis Milestones AND possess a 91+ PCT score**. This dual qualification is required for:
1. **Peer Hosting**: Hosting fellow community members in your home.
2. **Peer Stay**: Staying as a guest in host homes within the network.
3. **Leadership Candidacy**: Running for or holding an elected Community Leader position.
4. **Voting Rights**: Voting on Community Leaders and Community Loan Applications.

### Earning PCT & The Efficiency Gate
To prevent inflation and ensure active participation, your TDE distribution efficiency is tied to your Trust Score. 
*   **From the Beginning:** DSP and PCT are awarded instantly in real-time for all general activities (referrals, new chats, check-ins, creating events). You receive DSP + 1 PCT for each of these actions.
*   **Genesis Connection Milestones (The 10× Rule):** For the four Genesis connection milestones (Family, Friend, QR Exchange, and Vouched/Verified combined), you earn 1,000 DSP **instantly** per connection. However, the +10 PCT is awarded only at **10× intervals** (10, 100, 1,000).
*   **Profile Completion (The Genesis Exception):** The only milestone that does not pay out instantly is Profile Completion. It requires exactly 10 fields to fully complete, which then triggers a massive 10,000 DSP Genesis lump sum and +10 PCT all at once.

Your final Trust Score dictates your **Swap Rate** during TDE release.
*   *Formula*: `0% for scores 0-10; exact Score% for 11-90; 100% for 91-100`
*   A user with a score of 95 gets 100% conversion, a score of 51 gets 51%, and a score from 1-10 gets 0%.

### 🗳️ Governance Voting & Base-10 Penalties
*   **Daily Digest Alert**: Qualified voters receive **1 daily digest notification UNTIL they vote**. Once you vote or abstain, alerts for that ballot stop immediately.
*   **Leadership Cadence**: Members must vote for leadership across their hierarchical locations at least **1 time per year**.
*   **"Abstain / Reviewed" Option**: Ballots feature an "Abstain" choice so you can fulfill your voting requirement without forcing an uneducated vote.
*   **Missed Vote Slashing**:
    *   Standard Member: **-1 PCT point** per missed vote.
    *   Community Leader: **-10 PCT points** per missed vote (*leadership privileges are suspended until PCT is restored to 91+*).
*   **Civic Recovery Streak**: Voting on **10 consecutive proposals** awards a **+1 PCT Recovery Bonus**.
*   **Peer Hosting Feedback**: Mutual 5-star review = **+1 PCT point**; verified stay violation = **-10 PCT points**.

## 4. The 10,000 $DIASPORA Gate (TGE)
The final milestone is the **10,000 $DIASPORA Gate**. 
- **Proof of Commitment**: Once you reach 10,000 $DIASPORA and maintain a high Trust Score, you become eligible for the **Token Generation Event (TGE)**.
- **The Reset**: At the TGE, your activity is "crystallized." Your points will be swapped 1:1 for liquid tokens on the Coinbase L2 Base Blockchain, turning your social capital into real-world assets.

## 5. Frictionless Economy
All economic activities in Diaspora Nearby are **completely free of DSP fees**. You can:

*   **List and sell** on the Marketplace — no seller gate or listing fees
*   **Register a business** — no creation or product listing fees
*   **Create and attend events** — no event listing or attendance fees
*   **Host and travel** — hosting arrangements are fee-free

The DSP economy is sustained through **penalties** (see below), **protocol burns**, and **PCT efficiency** — not by taxing economic activity.
