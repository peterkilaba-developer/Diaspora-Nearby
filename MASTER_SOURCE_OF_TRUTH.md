### 3.5 Upward Cascading Liquidity Protocol for DDI Loans
When an approved DDI loan application exceeds the liquidity balance currently held in the local Town DDI Treasury, the protocol executes an **Upward Cascading Drawdown**:
1. **Town DDI Treasury** (First drawdown from local balance)
2. $\to$ **State Reserve Treasury** (Draws remaining shortfall from State Reserve backstop)
3. $\to$ **Country DDI Treasury** (Draws remaining shortfall from Country DDI pool)
4. $\to$ **Global Reserve** (Draws remaining shortfall from Global Reserve final backstop)

This multi-tier cascading mechanism guarantees that viable, community-approved local businesses and member enterprises are never blocked by temporary localized liquidity shortages.

## ⚡ 4. THE 3-STEP EPOCH LIFECYCLE: TGE, TDE & THE GREAT RESET
The protocol operates on a transparent 3-step cycle across 10 progressive scaling epochs:
1. **TGE (Token Generation Event — 1 per Epoch)**: Pre-mints and locks that epoch's fixed `$DIASPORA` supply on Ethereum Base Blockchain (Chain ID: 8453), guaranteeing 100% on-chain backing before distribution begins.
2. **TDE (Token Distribution Event — 10 Releases per Epoch)**: Every 10% milestone of distributed DSP converts off-chain DSP into on-chain `$DIASPORA` at each participant's PCT rate, while carrying over any unconverted DSP into the next TDE milestone (TDE 1–9).
3. **The Great Reset (TDE 10 — Epoch Completion)**: At TDE 10 (100% of the epoch's DSP target), any remaining unconverted off-chain DSP resets to 0 (and is burned on-chain to `0x000...dEaD`) while members keep all on-chain `$DIASPORA` tokens and PCT scores, launching the next 10x epoch.

### 4.1 Distributed DSP Trigger
TDEs trigger when cumulative distributed DSP crosses the next supply milestone (e.g., every 100,000 DSP in Epoch 1 toward the 1,000,000 DSP target). Verified population is tracked as an informational network-growth benchmark, not a gate.

### 4.2 The 10-TDE Event Cycle & Individual Pull Claims
Each Epoch consists of **10 Token Distribution Events (TDEs)**.
- Each event distributes **10%** of the Epoch's total supply target.
- **Individual Pull Claims**: Distribution is executed via an in-app **"Claim $DIASPORA"** button for each qualified user using cryptographic on-chain verification (Merkle pull claims), eliminating push-batch gas bottlenecks and ensuring infinite horizontal scalability. Earned tokens never expire.
- **System Exemption**: Founders, Liquidity Treasury, and Global Reserve convert at **100% efficiency**.

### 4.3 Universal Participation, Rolling Carry-Over & The Great Reset
- **Universal Inclusion**: Every registered member who completes the 6 Base PoH milestones participates in TDE conversions.
- **Rolling Carry-Over (TDE 1–9) & The Great Reset (TDE 10)**: During TDE 1 through TDE 9, only the converted portion (`DSP × PCT%`) is deducted from a member's off-chain `availablePoints`, while the unconverted DSP **carries over** into the next TDE milestone so members can increase their PCT score and convert it later. Only at **TDE 10 (The Great Reset — 100% of the Epoch target)** is any remaining unconverted DSP reset to **0** off-chain (and burned to `0x000...dEaD` on-chain), starting fresh accumulation for the next 10x Epoch.
