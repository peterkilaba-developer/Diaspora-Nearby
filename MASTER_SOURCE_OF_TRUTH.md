### 2.3 Mechanism 3: Proof of Community Trust (PCT) — The Trust Engine
**Status**: The Universal Reputation & Governance Filter.
**$DIASPORA** represents **Proof of Trust (PCT)**, which is derived directly from continuous Proof of Activity (P.O.A). A user's **Trust Score (0-100 PCT)** measures their verified community standing.

#### 🛡️ 1. The Full Prerequisite Gate (7 Genesis Milestones + 91+ PCT)
To unlock high-trust privileges, a member **must complete all 7 Genesis Milestones AND possess a 91+ PCT score**. This dual qualification is mandatory for:
1. **Peer Hosting**: Hosting fellow community members in your home.
2. **Peer Stay**: Staying as a guest in host homes within the network.
3. **Leadership Candidacy**: Running for or holding an elected Community Leader position.
4. **Voting Rights**: Voting on Community Leaders and Community Loan Applications.

#### ⏱️ 2. The 100-Day P.O.A Health Index & Status Pause
- **Active Standing**: Members must perform at least 1 Proof of Activity (P.O.A) action every **100 days** to maintain active 91+ PCT standing.
- **100-Day Status Pause**: Members can activate a 100-day freeze (max 1 per year) for travel, health, or off-grid periods to pause missed-vote penalties without forfeiting status.

#### 🗳️ 3. Mandatory Governance Voting & Base-10 Penalties
To maintain active governance and prevent community apathy:
- **Notification Stop Rule**: Qualified voters receive **1 daily digest notification UNTIL they vote**. Notifications stop immediately once pending ballots are cast or abstained.
- **Leadership Voting Cadence**: Members must vote for leadership across their hierarchical locations at least **1 time per year**.
- **Auto-Exemption**: Unqualified members (who have not completed the 7 Genesis Milestones) are exempt from missed-vote penalties.
- **"Abstain / Reviewed" Option**: Ballots feature an "Abstain" choice, allowing members to satisfy mandatory participation without forcing uneducated decisions.
- **Base-10 Missed-Vote Penalties**:
  - Standard Qualified Member: **-1 PCT point** per missed loan application or leadership election vote.
  - Community Leader: **-10 PCT points** per missed vote (*triggers automatic suspension of leadership privileges until PCT is restored to 91+*).
- **Civic Recovery Streak**: Voting on **10 consecutive proposals/elections** awards a **+1 PCT Recovery Bonus** (up to original baseline).

#### 🏠 4. Peer Hosting Feedback Loop
- **Successful Stay (Mutual 5-Star Reviews)**: Awards **+1 PCT point** to both host and guest.
- **Verified Misconduct Violation**: Triggers a **-10 PCT points penalty**, immediately dropping bad actors below the 91+ threshold.

#### 🏷️ 5. Dual Profile Trust Badges
- 🛡️ **Genesis Verified**: Completed all 7 Genesis Milestones.
- 👑 **PCT 91+ Sovereign**: Unlocked Peer Host, Peer Stay, & Leadership Candidacy.

- **The HTS Ledger (Human Trust System)**:
    - Points and Score are tracked in a verifiable ledger.
    - **Genesis Milestones (One-Time +10 PCT)**: Each Genesis category awards **+10 PCT once** upon reaching 10 unique verifications/fields. Max from Genesis Milestones = **70 PCT** (7 × 10).
    - **Connection DSP**: For connections (Family, Friends, QR, Trust), you earn 1,000 DSP **instantly** per connection.
    - **Profile Completion (The Genesis Exception)**: Pays nothing until fully complete (10 fields), then releases 10,000 DSP + 10 PCT at once.
    - **Trust Slider Staking (Two-Sided Prediction Market)**: Users rate others via a 1-100 trust slider (No Trust 1-10 locks stake against; Full Trust 91-100 locks stake for).
    - **Net Vouches**: Every +10 Net Vouches = **+1 PCT**. Every -10 Net = **-1 PCT**.

## 🏦 3. ECONOMIC ARCHITECTURE: THE UBUNTU PROTOCOL (1:1 EXPANSION)
We utilize a **Constant-Earning, Variable-Scarcity** model based on the philosophy of *Ubuntu* ("I am because we are"). This philosophy powers both the 1:1 Matching Expansion and the separate Community Emergency Fund.

### 3.1 The Ubuntu Protocol (1:1 Expansion)
For every **1.0 DSP** earned by a user through PSA, the system mints an additional **1.0 DSP** in "Matching Supply" for the hierarchical dual geographic community treasuries.
- **User Reward (50%)**: Direct liquidity for the contributor.
- **System Match (50%)**: Collective wealth divided into 10 Slots.

### 3.2 System Match Allocation (10-Slot Rule)
For every $1 earned by a user, the protocol mints $1 which is distributed as follows:
- **10% to Founders & Core Team**: Fixed 100% Efficiency.
- **10% to Investors & Partners**: Strategic liquidity and growth partners.
- **10% Fixed Burn**: Immediate deflationary pressure.
- **70% Community Sovereignty**: (7 Slots) Subject to CPoCT efficiency. Any unassigned slot is BURNED.

#### The 7 Community Slots (Tributes)
1. **Global Community**: Wealth for large-scale catastrophes and emergencies.
2. **Home Continent**: Large-scale regional infrastructure and DDI.
3. **Home Country**: National heritage and DDI Projects.
4. **Home City**: Local community infrastructure and DDI.
5. **Host Continent**: Growth in the Diaspora's new home.
6. **Host Country**: Integration and host-nation impact projects.
7. **Host City**: Immediate neighborhood connectivity and DDI.

*Note: The **State/Province/Region** level is deprecated to maintain a streamlined 7-tier community hierarchy.*

### 3.3 The Constant-10 Rule & Overflow
- **Fixed Infrastructure Burn**: Exactly 10% of every matching expansion is destroyed.
- **Constant Occupation**: All 10 slots (Founders, Investors, Burn, 7 Communities) are always filled.
- **Global Overflow**: If a user has not joined a specific regional tier (e.g., Home City), that unit is automatically directed to the **Global Community** treasury. This ensures the 1:1 match is never broken and community wealth is always preserved.

## ⚡ 4. THE GREAT RESET: TOKEN GENERATION EVENT (TGE)
TGE is the "Crystalization" event where off-chain DSP points become on-chain liquid $DIASPORA currency.

### 4.1 Distributed DSP Trigger
TGE triggers when cumulative distributed DSP crosses the next supply milestone
(e.g., 1,000,000 DSP for Epoch 1). Verified population is tracked as an
informational network-growth benchmark, but it does not gate TGE execution.

### 4.2 The 10-TGE Event Cycle
Each Epoch consists of **10 TGE events**.
- Each event distributes **10%** of the Epoch's total supply target.
- This creates 10 specific- **Wealth Windows**: TGE events happen in 10% increments of the supply milestone.
- **System Exemption**: Founders convert at **100% efficiency**, reflecting their fixed commitment to platform infrastructure.

### 4.3 Universal Participation
- **Universal Inclusion**: Every registered user, regardless of their personal balance or verification status, participates in the TGE conversion once the global triggers are met.
- This ensures absolute inclusion for the entire Diaspora community while maintaining the integrity of the network expansion.

## 📈 6. TOKENOMICS VALUATION STRATEGY (Internal GDP)
Unlike traditional speculative tokens, $DIASPORA's base value is anchored to the real economic activity within the app.

### 6.1 The Valuation Formula
- **Internal Floor Price**: `Price = (Internal GDP / Circulating Supply) / Velocity_Adjustment`
- **Internal GDP**: The total cumulative volume (in $DIASPORA) of all transfers, remittances, and trades processed through the protocol.
- **Public Projection**: To reflect future utility and network effects, the estimated market value is calculated as `Internal Price * 3.5` (Target Multiplier).

### 6.2 Bilateral Corridors
The system tracks **Corridor Strength** between host and home countries (e.g., UK-Tanzania).
- **Demand Pressure**: High-volume corridors increase the local valuation for those specific communities, reflecting a "Remittance Hub" premium.
- **Network Resilience**: Diversified corridors reduce volatility by spreading demand across multiple global economic paths.

$DIASPORA is permanently removed from circulation through **community-driven penalties** and **protocol-level burns**. The economy is **frictionless** — no fees are charged for economic activities like marketplace listings, event creation, business registration, or hosting.

### 7.1 Frictionless Economy
All in-app economic activities are **completely free of DSP fees**:
- **Marketplace**: No seller gate, listing, buy, apply, or hire fees
- **Events**: No event listing or attendance fees
- **Business**: No organization creation or product listing fees
- **Hosting**: No traveler daily fees — hosting is free
