## ⚙️ 2. THE THREE PROTOCOLS (PoI, PoA, PoCT)
The economy is powered by three interlinking validation protocols.

### 2.1 Mechanism 1: Proof of Identity (PoI)
**Status**: The Gatekeeper.
Before a user can extract value (TGE), they must prove their humanity via a **QR Identity Exchange**.

- **Level 1: Profile Completeness**: Filling out the core profile fields.
- **Level 2: Social Entropy (Multi-User Verification)**: 
    - The system enforces a **"One field per verifier"** constraint.
    - To be fully verified, exactly **10** unique peers must validate different facets of your life. 
    - *Why?* This prevents collusive bot-farms from "auto-verifying" each other. Algorithms cannot easily fake the messy, diverse validations of real humans.
- **Level 3: Physical Presence (The QR Handshake)**: 
    - A cryptographic handshake performed via QR code when two users meet in person.
    - This is the "Gold Standard" of identity, awarding the highest trust and point bonuses (10,000 $DIASPORA each).

### 2.3 Mechanism 3: Proof of Community Trust (PoCT)
**Status**: The Filter.
A user's **Trust Score (0-100)** is a mathematical representation of their community standing.

- **The HTS Ledger (Human Trust System)**:
    - Points and Score are tracked in a verifiable ledger.
    - **Genesis Milestones (One-Time +10 PoCT)**: Each of the 5 Genesis categories awards **+10 PoCT once** when reaching **10 unique users/fields** (Family, Friends, QR Exchange, Vouch/Verify, Profile). After the first 10, activities continue to earn $DIASPORA but **no more PoCT**. Max from Genesis Milestones = **50 PoCT** (5 × 10).
    - **Connection DSP**: For connections (Family, Friends, QR, Trust), you earn 1,000 $DIASPORA **instantly** per connection, regardless of milestone status.
    - **Profile Completion (The Genesis Exception)**: The only milestone that delays both $DIASPORA and PoCT. It pays nothing until fully complete (10 fields), then releases 10,000 $DIASPORA + 10 PoCT at once.
    - **Trust Slider Staking (The Only Ongoing PoCT Earner)**: Users rate others via a 0-100 trust slider. Rating anyone earns **1,000 $DIASPORA** (vouch reward). Giving **full trust (>90)** locks that same 1,000 $DIASPORA as a stake — no additional cost. Every **10 unique full-trust voters** awards **+1 PoCT** to the trusted user. **Staker Economics**: For each subsequent rater who also gives full trust, the staker earns +10 $DIASPORA. For each rater who gives ≤90, 10 $DIASPORA is burned from the stake. If the stake reaches 0, the full-trust vouch is auto-removed. The staker can exit anytime by sliding below 91 to reclaim remaining balance. Rating ≤90 earns the 1,000 $DIASPORA free and clear.
    - **HTS Penalties**: Penalties deduct **-1 PoCT per 10 occurrences** (e.g., blocked by 10 users = -1 PoCT). Referrals, events, and verified solutions earn $DIASPORA only, **not PoCT**.
- **The Genesis Gate**:
    - Every user must perform at least one **QR Identity Exchange** to prove their humanity and qualify for their first TGE conversion.
- **The Decile Efficiency Gate**:
    - Your score determines your **Swap Rate** at the Token Generation Event.
    - *Formula*: `Efficiency = (Score / 10).ceil() / 10.0`
    - A user with a score of 95 gets 100% (1:1), while a user with a score of 51 gets only 60% conversion.

## 🏦 3. ECONOMIC ARCHITECTURE (1:1 EXPANSION)
We utilize a **Constant-Earning, Variable-Scarcity** model.

### 3.1 The 1:1 Expansion Rule
For every **1.0 $DIASPORA** earned by a user through PoA, the system mints an additional **1.0 $DIASPORA** in "Matching Supply" for treasuries.
- **User Reward (50%)**: Direct liquidity for the contributor.
- **System Match (50%)**: Collective wealth divided into 10 Slots.

### 3.2 System Match Allocation (10-Slot Rule)
For every $1 earned by a user, the protocol mints $1 which is distributed as follows:
- **10% to Founders & Core Team**: Fixed 100% Efficiency.
- **10% to Investors & Partners**: Strategic liquidity and growth partners.
- **10% Fixed Burn**: Immediate deflationary pressure.
- **70% Community Sovereignty**: (7 Slots) Subject to CPoCT efficiency. Any unassigned slot is BURNED.

#### The 7 Community Slots (Tributes)
1. **Global Community**: Wealth for large-scale catastrophes and emergencies.
2. **Home Continent**: Large-scale regional infrastructure and DDI.
3. **Home Country**: National heritage and DDI Projects.
4. **Home City**: Local community infrastructure and DDI.
5. **Host Continent**: Growth in the Diaspora's new home.
6. **Host Country**: Integration and host-nation impact projects.
7. **Host City**: Immediate neighborhood connectivity and DDI.

*Note: The **State/Province/Region** level is deprecated to maintain a streamlined 7-tier community hierarchy.*

### 3.3 The Constant-10 Rule & Overflow
- **Fixed Infrastructure Burn**: Exactly 10% of every matching expansion is destroyed.
- **Constant Occupation**: All 10 slots (Founders, Investors, Burn, 7 Communities) are always filled.
- **Global Overflow**: If a user has not joined a specific regional tier (e.g., Home City), that unit is automatically directed to the **Global Community** treasury. This ensures the 1:1 match is never broken and community wealth is always preserved.

## ⚡ 4. THE GREAT RESET: TOKEN GENERATION EVENT (TGE)
TGE is the "Crystalization" event where off-chain $DIASPORA points become on-chain liquid currency.

### 4.1 Dual-Trigger Evolution
To ensure the network is robust, TGE only triggers when BOTH metrics are met:
1. **Supply Milestone**: Cumulative $DIASPORA supply distributed reaches the Epoch's target (e.g., 1,000,000 $DIASPORA for Epoch 1).
2. **User Milestone**: Required Verified Population reached (e.g., 100 Verified Humans for Epoch 1).

### 4.2 The 10-TGE Event Cycle
Each Epoch consists of **10 TGE events**.
- Each event distributes **10%** of the Epoch's total supply target.
- This creates 10 specific- **Wealth Windows**: TGE events happen in 10% increments of the supply milestone.
- **System Exemption**: Founders convert at **100% efficiency**, reflecting their fixed commitment to platform infrastructure.

### 4.3 Universal Participation
- **Universal Inclusion**: Every registered user, regardless of their personal balance or verification status, participates in the TGE conversion once the global triggers are met.
- This ensures absolute inclusion for the entire Diaspora community while maintaining the integrity of the network expansion.

## 🤝 8. BILATERAL CHAMBERS OF COMMERCE
To foster trusted cross-border trade and diaspora business integrations, the protocol establishes official bilateral **Chambers of Commerce** for each corridor.

### 8.1 Dual-Chamber Integration
* **Connecting Network**: The Chamber of Commerce of a host country (e.g., US Chamber of Commerce) is integrated directly with the Home Country's Diaspora Chamber of Commerce (e.g., Tanzania Diaspora Chamber of Commerce).

### 8.2 Lived-Experience Representation
* **Dedicated Representatives**: The Home Country Diaspora Chamber houses dedicated representatives responsible for each host country where its diaspora resides.
* **Lived-In Requirement**: Every representative **must have lived in the specific host country** they represent (e.g., a rep for the TZ-US corridor must have lived in the United States).
* **Understanding Business Cultures**: This strict criteria guarantees that representatives possess firsthand, deep understanding of both countries' business cultures, trade regulations, market dynamics, and operational landscapes, facilitating frictionless bilateral trade and joint ventures.

### 8.3 Host-Country Native Access (B2B Bridge)
* **Open Access Policy**: Native individuals and businesses established in the host country (who are not part of the diaspora themselves) are fully permitted to register, create verified business profiles, and access any bilateral chamber corridor they wish to do business with.
* **Corridor Discovery**: Once authenticated, host-country native businesses can search the global chamber registry and join corridors (e.g., a native US logistics firm or manufacturer joining the TZ-US Chamber) to pitch services, seek trade partners, source raw materials, or propose joint ventures directly using the smart contract escrow pipeline.

### 8.4 Unified Governance & DDI Integration (Efficiency Lock)
* **Unified Leadership**: To eliminate coordination friction and administrative gridlock, the elected regional Community Leaders (President, Treasurer, and Secretary) automatically serve as the ex-officio Executive Board of the Bilateral Chamber of Commerce.
* **Appointed Portfolios**: The Executive Board appoints the specialized Corridor Representatives (meeting the PoCT 91+ and lived-in requirements) to act as the operational, due-diligence, and dispute-resolution arm of the chamber.
* **Seamless DDI/FDDI Integration**: The Chamber of Commerce acts as the primary "Deal Sourcing and Vetting" engine. Sourced investments and loans are audited by the appointed reps, approved by the ex-officio board, and then passed to the general community for the final democratic vote (51% DDI or 67% FDDI), ensuring absolute institutional alignment.
