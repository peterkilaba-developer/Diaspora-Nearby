# Privacy Policy for Diaspora Nearby

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-02

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Diaspora Nearby** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

We utilize Google Firebase and other third-party services to provide our services. We may collect the following types of information:

### A. Personal Data
When you register or use the App, we collect personal information such as:
*   **Identity Data:** Name, username, profile picture.
*   **Contact Data:** Email address, phone number (for authentication via Firebase Auth).
*   **Demographic Data:** Organization affiliations, community groups.

### B. Location Data
**Crucial for App Functionality:** "Diaspora Nearby" is a location-based social network.
*   We collect your **precise** and **coarse location** data to connect you with community members, events, and organizations near you.
*   This data is used to display your distance from others and to filter content based on proximity.
*   You can control location access through your device settings at any time.

### C. Device & Technical Data
*   Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
*   IP address and network information.
*   App usage data (screens visited, interactions) via Firebase Analytics to improve user experience.

### D. User Generated Content
*   **Media:** Photos and videos you share/upload (accessing your Camera or Photo Library).
*   **Audio:** Voice notes or audio recordings (accessing your Microphone).
*   **Messages:** Text and private messages sent between users.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
*   **Provide Core Services:** specificially, to find and display users, groups, and events near your current location.
*   **Account Management:** To create, authenticate, and manage your account.
*   **Communication:** To send you updates, security alerts, and support messages (via Push Notifications).
*   **Improvement:** To analyze usage trends and improve App functionality.
*   **Safety:** To monitor and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.

## 4. Permissions We Request

The App may request access to the following sensitive permissions:
*   **Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):** To provide nearby community features.
*   **Camera:** To take profile photos, scan QR codes, or capture content for posts.
*   **Microphone:** To record voice messages.
*   **Storage / Photos:** To upload existing images or save data.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not** sell your personal data. We may share information only in the following situations:
*   **Service Providers:** With third-party vendors (e.g., Google Firebase, Google Maps) who perform services for us (data hosting, analytics, maps).
*   **Functionality:** Your profile info (Name, Avatar, Location proximity) is visible to other users of the App as part of the social networking experience.
*   **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect rights and safety.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
*   We retain your data as long as your account is active.
*   **Account Deletion:** You have the right to delete your account and associated data. You can request account deletion within the App settings or by contacting us. Upon deletion, your data is removed from our active databases, subject to legitimate legal or business retention needs.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
