The Dual Geographic Identity: Living Between Two Worlds

Every member of Diaspora Nearby possesses a dual geographic identity: your Home Country (roots, lineage & return) and your Host Country (presence, work & mobility). Everything we build — from nearby discovery and real-time alerts to sovereign trust, mutual safety pools, peer homestays, and 1:1 matching community treasuries — flows directly from this foundational reality.

The Trust Slider — Stake on the People You Believe In

Long-press any message to rate the sender's trust on a 1–100 slider. In DM chats the slider appears once — a one-time trust signal. In community, event, group, and organization chats, ratings are continuous: your latest rating overwrites previous ones as trust evolves. Every first-time rating earns the rater 1,000 DSP.

Score 1–10 — No Trust (Staked Against)

You're actively betting against this person's trust. Your 1,000 DSP is staked as collateral. You earn rewards if others agree, and burn your stake if the community defends them.

Score 11–90 — Standard Trust (Free Signal)

A neutral interaction rating. Earn your 1,000 DSP and keep it free and clear. No collateral locked, no ongoing risk. Has zero impact on existing stakers.

Score 91–100 — Full Trust (Staked For)

You're publicly backing this person. Your 1,000 DSP is staked. You earn rewards when the community agrees, and burn stake when they vote No Trust.
