Continental Treasuries

The Sovereign Bond Engine: Sustainable Platform Revenue & Government Partnerships

Continental Treasuries (Home & Host Continent) convert member tribute into on-chain $DIASPORA tokens on Base scaled by the Average Proof of Community Trust (APCT). The protocol manages these continental funds in a 100% Sovereign Bond Allocation: 100% of funds purchase sovereign government bonds to generate sustainable platform revenue and establish deep sovereign partnerships.

100% Sovereign Bond Allocation

100% of continental funds are used and managed by the protocol to purchase sovereign government bonds and Treasury Bills. Allocations are distributed pro-rata based on each country's contribution (e.g., Tanzania's contribution into the African Home Treasury buys Bank of Tanzania bonds; host continent funds buy bonds like US Treasuries).

Government Integration & Trust

Governments view these treasuries as an almost guaranteed, reliable, low-interest source of sovereign funding for their budgets. This turns national central banks and finance ministries into willing partners eager to integrate Diaspora Nearby into national economic, banking, and payment systems (e.g., TIPS in Tanzania, FedNow).

Sustainable Platform Revenue

Coupon interest and bond yields from sovereign government securities generate sustainable protocol revenue to maintain operations, engineering, and infrastructure long-term.

Sovereign Reserve Security

By committing 100% of continental treasury capital to sovereign government bonds, the protocol establishes direct institutional alignment with central banks, securing continental reserves with sovereign credit.

How Pro-Rata Continental Allocation Works (Tanzania Example)

1. Contribution Attribution: As Tanzanian diaspora members interact, complete milestones, and trade, their 1:1 Ubuntu match builds the African Home Continent Treasury. The protocol tracks Tanzania's proportional share.

2. APCT Conversion at TGE: At token distribution events, off-chain DSP is converted into on-chain $DIASPORA tokens on Base scaled by the Average Proof of Community Trust (APCT) of active members.

3. Pro-Rata Bond Purchase: If Tanzania contributed 25% of the African Continental Treasury, 100% of Tanzania's share (25% of the total continental pool) purchases Bank of Tanzania sovereign bonds and Treasury Bills.

4. Mutual Win: The government gains non-predatory sovereign budget funding; coupon yields provide sustainable platform revenue to maintain protocol infrastructure; and the national economy benefits from direct diaspora-backed sovereign capital investment!

Peter Swai

Founder & Architect — The 25-Year Diaspora Journey & The Return Home

Peter moved from Tanzania to the United States in 1998, landing first in San Francisco, California with two friends. Within a few months, he faced the reality millions of immigrants know intimately: without a job, the high cost of living and college tuition became unaffordable.

He relocated south to Santa Monica in Southern California, but living costs were equally crushing. Searching for stability and opportunity, his diaspora odyssey took him to Iowa, then to Washington, D.C., then to Phoenix, Arizona, and eventually back to Iowa where he lives today.

Across every relocation, his social circle reset to zero. He had no local credit history, no established trust, and no easy way to find verified people from home. But after over 25 years in the United States, Peter is now actively preparing his return home to Tanzania—living the other half of the diaspora equation.

Peter's vision is that Diaspora Nearby must not only benefit newcomers arriving in unfamiliar cities, but crucially prepare and empower those returning home. By carrying your verified trust, sovereign community treasuries, and Home DDI member loans wherever you travel, you never start from zero—whether landing in a new host city or making your triumphant return home.
