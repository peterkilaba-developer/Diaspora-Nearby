3. DSP vs $DIASPORA — Two Systems, One Journey

DSP (Diaspora Social Points) and $DIASPORA are intentionally decoupled. Understanding the difference is essential to understanding the economic model.

Diaspora Social Points (DSP)

  • Off-chain trust score stored in Firestore
  • Earned through daily social activity (verifying, vouching, messaging, transacting)
  • Tracks your social contribution to the community
  • Accumulates until the next TDE milestone is triggered
  • The Ubuntu Protocol (1:1 matching) — for every 1 DSP earned, another 1 DSP is created for geographic community treasuries

$DIASPORA Token

  • On-chain ERC-20 on Ethereum Base Blockchain — a real, liquid asset you own
  • Pre-minted and locked per epoch by the TGE Controller contract
  • Conversion rate at TDE depends on your PCT (Proof of Community Trust) score
  • Fully verifiable on Basescan
  • Burns are permanent — sent to address zero, verifiable on-chain

TDE Conversion: A TDE fires when globally distributed DSP crosses the next supply milestone. Member counts are informational growth benchmarks, not TDE gates. Eligible off-chain DSP converts into liquid $DIASPORA tokens at each participant's PCT efficiency rate.

Proof of Community Trust (PCT)

Your PCT score (0-100) dictates how efficiently your DSP converts to $DIASPORA at each TDE. Scores 1-10 convert at 0%, 11-90 convert at exact percentage, and 91-100 convert at 100%.

The foundation of trust is established across the 4 Pillars of Proof of Humanity (PoH), each milestone worth 10,000 DSP and 10 PCT (70,000 DSP and 70 PCT Base Max). Upon completing all 7 PoH milestones, the protocol automatically executes the Proof of Humanity Genesis Distribution: converting 7,000 DSP into 7,000 real on-chain $DIASPORA tokens on Ethereum Base Blockchain by Coinbase (the only pre-TDE on-chain distribution), while the remaining 63,000 DSP converts at the scheduled global TDE epoch:

  • 1. Current Location Transparency: Real-time live location. Turning it off permanently forfeits the 10 PCT and burns the awarded 10,000 DSP.
  • 2. Profile Completion: Complete all 10 required fields for 10,000 DSP and 10 PCT.
  • 3. QR Identity Exchanges: 10 unique in-person exchanges for 10,000 DSP and 10 PCT (1,000 DSP per scan 11+).
  • 4. Family Connections (Circle of Trust): 10 verified family connections (10,000 DSP and 10 PCT). Requires bilateral chat history; mutual slashing contagion applies.
  • 5. Friend Connections (Circle of Trust): 10 verified friend connections (10,000 DSP and 10 PCT). Requires bilateral chat history; mutual slashing contagion applies.
  • 6. Verified by Peers: 10 unique peer profile field verifications (10,000 DSP and 10 PCT). Verifiers must be in Circle of Trust or QR-met with bilateral chat.
  • 7. Ubuntu Emergency Fund: Contribution to the mutual catastrophe protection pool for 10,000 DSP and 10 PCT.

This ensures qualified community businesses always access necessary funding.

Continental Treasuries (50/50 Sovereign Bond Engine & Zero-Fee Revenue)

Continental Treasuries (Home Continent and Host Continent) convert accumulated off-chain DSP into on-chain $DIASPORA tokens on Base at TGE/TDE scaled by the Average Proof of Community Trust (APCT) of their active members. The protocol manages these continental funds under a 50/50 Sovereign Split:

  • 50% Sovereign Bond Allocation: Managed by the protocol to purchase sovereign government bonds and Treasury Bills pro-rata by each country's contribution (e.g., Tanzania's contribution into the African Home Treasury buys Bank of Tanzania bonds; host continent funds buy bonds like US Treasuries). This provides national governments with guaranteed, reliable, low-interest budget funding, motivating central banks to integrate Diaspora Nearby into national economic and payment switches (like TIPS in Tanzania, FedNow).
  • 100% Platform Revenue (Zero Fees to Users): Because Diaspora Nearby operates as a frictionless economy charging 0% fees to members, all bond interest and coupon yields flow exclusively to Diaspora Nearby to sustainably fund servers, engineering, and operations.
  • 50% Liquid $DIASPORA Backstop: Retained directly on Base in liquid $DIASPORA tokens as the regional liquidity backstop for Town and Country member DDI loans under the Upward Cascading Liquidity Protocol.

7. Dynamic Valuation Model

$DIASPORA's value is not speculative — it's anchored to real economic activity. The implied token price is determined by a unified dynamic price floor model that combines network growth factors with direct fiat GDP backing.

Unified Dynamic Price Floor Formula

Dynamic Price Floor = [Base × (1 + NetworkFactor) × (1 + DeflationFactor) × EpochMultiplier] + (Total Fiat GDP ÷ Circulating Supply)

Factor Breakdown

  • Base Price: Fixed starting constant ($0.001 per token).
  • Network Factor (N): Based on Metcalfe's Law — value scales with user adoption, normalized logarithmically. More active users = higher network baseline.
  • Deflation Factor (D): Measures the burn ratio (totalBurned ÷ totalMinted). As more tokens are permanently burned, scarcity increases.
  • Epoch Multiplier (E): Progressive scarcity multiplier — Epoch 1 = 1.1x, Epoch 2 = 1.2x, Epoch 3 = 1.3x, etc. Formula: 1 + (epoch * 0.1).
  • Fiat GDP Floor (G): Direct additive fiat economic backing. Every flight booked, product sold, and service hired adds real fiat value directly to the token floor. Formula: Total Fiat GDP ÷ Circulating Supply.

This unified price floor ensures that $DIASPORA always has a non-zero starting baseline anchored to network growth at Day 1, which strictly rises as platform fiat GDP accumulates.

9. Security Architecture

UUPS Proxy Pattern

Contracts are upgradeable via UUPS proxies with OpenZeppelin's battle-tested libraries, allowing bug fixes while preserving state.

Role-Based Access

Admin, Minter, Upgrader, and TGE Oracle roles are separated. No single key controls everything.

On-Chain Verification

All minting, locking, and distribution events are recorded on-chain. Anyone can verify on the blockchain.

Immutable Burns

Burned tokens are sent to the zero address — permanently removed from circulation, verifiable on-chain.

Smart Wallet Architecture

Users interact with the blockchain through Self-Custodial Smart Wallets based on Account Abstraction. These wallets are deterministically generated from phone numbers using CREATE2, enabling gasless transactions where the platform sponsors gas fees on behalf of users.

11. Community Governance

Governance in Diaspora Nearby is merit-based, not plutocratic. Only community members who meet strict eligibility requirements can participate in governance decisions.

Governance Eligibility

  • 100 days active participation minimum
  • Must be an admin of a 100+ member group
  • PCT score ≥ 91 (Proof of Community Trust)
  • Staking requirement: PCT score must stay above 90 (at 91+)

Governance Types

  • Host Town DDI: 51% vote required, 10-day window — members can pool collateral to fund up to $1,000,000 with 10% $DIASPORA collateral. Applicant must live in the host town they apply from.
  • Home Town DDI: 51% vote required, 10-day window — members can pool collateral to fund up to $1,000,000 with 10% $DIASPORA collateral. Applicant must live in the home town they apply from.
  • Host Country DDI (Exceeding $1,000,000+): 67% supermajority, 100-day window — for $1,000,000+ enterprise acquisitions, commercial real estate, or any community-voted investment in the host nation.
  • Home Country DDI (Exceeding $1,000,000+): 67% supermajority, 100-day window — for $1,000,000+ infrastructure, agriculture, or any community-voted sovereign investment in the home country.

No Token Voting: Governance is based on trust and community contribution, not token holdings. This prevents whale dominance and ensures decisions are made by active, trusted community members.

12. Liquidity Treasury

The Liquidity Treasury allocates 5% of total epoch supply to provide DEX liquidity for the $DIASPORA ecosystem. Rather than private sales, SAFT offerings, or VC allocations, this approach gives everyone equal access to $DIASPORA on decentralized exchanges.

How It Works

Each epoch has 10 TDE (Token Distribution Event) milestones. The TDE fires each time 10% of the epoch DSP is distributed, releasing that decile of the epoch's DEX liquidity supply. This prevents users from having to wait for the entire epoch to finish to reap the rewards. For example, during the first epoch (1,000,000 DSP supply), a TDE occurs at every 100,000 DSP distributed. In epoch 2 (10,000,000 DSP supply), a TDE occurs at every 1,000,000 DSP distributed.

Why Open Market Instead of Private Sales

  • No VCs, no private rounds — anyone can access $DIASPORA on equal terms via DEX
  • Only 5% of total epoch supply — a modest, controlled release
  • Earned tokens stay held — users who earn through community participation tend to hold, creating natural scarcity and making the Liquidity Treasury a key accessible source
  • No securities complexity — the Liquidity Treasury provides protocol-managed liquidity, not a securities offering

Community-First: The Liquidity Treasury ensures that token access isn't gatekept by accreditation requirements or minimum investment thresholds. Anyone, anywhere, can participate through a decentralized exchange.
