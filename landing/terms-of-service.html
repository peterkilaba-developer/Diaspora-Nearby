5. Child Safety Standards & Zero-Tolerance Policy Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Diaspora Nearby (developed and operated by Peter Swai) maintains an absolute, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Protecting children and preventing any form of minor exploitation on our platform is a top priority.

  • Explicit Prohibition of CSAE and CSAM: We strictly prohibit the creation, uploading, sharing, distribution, possession, solicitation, or promotion of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or any content, communication, or activity that facilitates or depicts Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). This includes, without limitation:
    • Any visual depiction (photos, videos, AI-generated or computer-generated imagery, or illustrations) of sexually explicit conduct involving a minor (anyone under the age of 18).
    • Predatory behavior, online grooming, inappropriate interaction with minors, sexual solicitation of minors, child sextortion, or child sex trafficking.
    • Sharing links, external URLs, invite codes, or references to off-platform resources that host or promote CSAM or CSAE.
  • In-App Reporting & User Feedback Mechanism: Diaspora Nearby provides built-in, easily accessible in-app reporting tools across the entire application. Users can report any suspected child safety concern, CSAE/CSAM violation, abusive message, profile, community chat, or post directly within the App by tapping the Report / Block User action on any user profile, direct message, community post, or event, or via Settings → Help & Support. Reports are prioritized and reviewed 24/7 by our Trust & Safety team.
  • Enforcement & Method for Addressing CSAM: Upon detecting or receiving a report of actual or suspected CSAM or CSAE:
    • We immediately remove and disable access to the offending content.
    • We immediately and permanently terminate the account(s) of any user involved, blocking their phone number, email, device identifiers, and wallet addresses from re-registering on Diaspora Nearby.
    • We preserve relevant digital evidence and account records in accordance with applicable legal obligations.
  • Mandatory Reporting to NCMEC & Law Enforcement Compliance: Diaspora Nearby complies with all applicable regional, national, and international child safety laws and regulations (including 18 U.S.C. § 2258A). We promptly report all apparent or suspected instances of CSAM and CSAE to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline (www.cybertipline.org) as well as to relevant national and regional law enforcement authorities worldwide.
  • Designated Child Safety Point of Contact: If you encounter any suspected CSAE/CSAM on Diaspora Nearby or have questions regarding our Child Safety Standards, please contact our designated Child Safety Point of Contact immediately:
    • App Name: Diaspora Nearby (com.diasporanearby.diasporanearby)

    • Child Safety Email: support@diasporanearby.com
