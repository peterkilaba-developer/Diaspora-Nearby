Why Open Market

Community-First, Open Access

Instead of private sales, token presales, or securities offerings — the Liquidity Treasury deploys directly to DEX pools. Equal access for everyone.

No Private Sales, No VCs, No Minimums

Trade any amount on equal terms

There are no private rounds, pre-sales, VC allocations, or lot-size requirements. Liquidity goes to Aerodrome DEX — anyone can trade any amount on equal terms.

Only 5% of Total Epoch Supply

Modest, controlled release

The Liquidity Treasury represents just 5% of total epoch supply (10% of the matched half). Combined with the fact that most users hold their earned tokens, this creates naturally tight supply and healthy price discovery.

Earned Tokens ≠ Sold Tokens

Natural scarcity

Users earn tokens through community participation, creating strong attachment and holding behavior. This means circulating sell-side supply stays naturally low, making the Liquidity Treasury one of the few accessible sources.
