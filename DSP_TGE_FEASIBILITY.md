# $DSP TGE FEASIBILITY STUDY

Based on the system architecture we've built and the current reward schedules, here is an estimation of whether a 1 Billion $DSP TGE is realistic.

---

## 1. User Growth & Timeline (Velocity)
We have designed the PoA (Proof of Activity) rewards to be significant enough to drive engagement but protected by decay and trust scores.

*   **Average User Earning**: An active member (Daily Open + Community Messaging + Referrals/Relationships) can realistically earn **500–1,500 DSP per day**.
*   **Annual Accumulation**: ~200,000 to 400,000 DSP per year for a single high-activity user.
*   **Network Critical Mass**:
    - To reach 1 Billion DSP with **10,000 active users**, it would take roughly **9–12 months**.
    - With **50,000 active users**, it would take roughly **2–3 months**.

**Verdict**: The 1B DSP trigger is an excellent "Critical Mass" filter. It ensures the network is not "hollow" when the token launches; there will be thousands of real, trusted humans already using the system.

## 2. Market Capitalization & Valuation
If the TGE mints tokens based on user activity (Total = User * 2):

*   **At $0.01 per token**: Total Market Cap = **$10 Million** (for 1B supply).
*   **At $0.50 per token**: Total Market Cap = **$500 Million**.
*   **The Scaling Reality**: With a **1 Quadrillion (1QD)** supply cap, $DSP is designed to support not just human users, but millions of AI agents and machine-to-machine transactions as the ecosystem matures through Epochs 8, 9, and 10.

**Verdict**: The shift to a **50/50 split** simplifies the narrative and increases user rewards compared to the previous model. The 10-Epoch structure provides a clear, long-term roadmap for global scale.

---

## 3. The "Trust" Filter (Scarcity)
The requirement for a **PoCT Efficiency Scale** is the secret weapon here.

*   **Selective Conversion**: Not all points convert at 1:1. Users with lower trust scores receive fewer tokens, effectively "burning" potential supply.
*   **Genesis Gate**: The **10,000 DSP** milestone ensures that recipients have a minimum skin in the game.
*   **System Treasury Pro-Rata**: The 50% system share (Country, Continent, Ecosystem, etc.) is only minted when users earn, ensuring that institutional wealth is directly tied to community activity.

**Yes, it is highly realistic.**

The evolution to the **"Power of 10"** model makes the system more mathematically sound and easier for users to understand (10 Epochs, 10 TGE steps, 50/50 split). This clarity is essential for driving the massive growth required to reach the later epochs.
