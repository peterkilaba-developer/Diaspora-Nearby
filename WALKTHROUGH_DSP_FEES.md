# Walkthrough: Diaspora Nearby — Frictionless Sovereign Economy

The Diaspora Nearby economy is entirely frictionless — users list businesses, create events, post marketplace listings, and participate in all activities without any platform transaction fees or taxes.

## Design Philosophy

The guiding principle is: **frictionless activity fosters collective prosperity**. Free listing, buying, selling, and participating maximizes real economic volume. Value and deflationary mechanics arise from organic participation, protocol expansion burns, and community character accountability:

## Value Sinks (Non-Fee Mechanisms)

### 1. Community-Driven Penalties
| Penalty | Amount | Trigger |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Chat Ad Penalty | −1,000 DSP | 10 unique spam reports |
| Spam Report | −100 DSP | Per-report deduction |
| Relationship Rejection | −1,000 DSP | Rejected request |
| Event No-Show | −1,000 DSP | Confirmed absence |
| Vouching Bad Actor | −1,000 DSP | Vouched for a fraudster |
| Misinformation | −1,000 DSP | Community-flagged content |
| Deceptive Org | −10,000 DSP | Fraudulent business |
| Confirmed Fraud | −10,000 DSP | Proven fraud |
| Unresponsive Liaison | −100 DSP | Failed community duties |
| Location Disabled Inside App | Burn 10k DSP | Deliberate in-app toggle OFF |

### 2. Protocol-Level Burns
- **10% fixed expansion burn** (on new DSP minted)
- **PCT efficiency burn** (unconverted portion burned to 0x...dEaD; users claim earned tokens anytime)

## Fiat GDP Tracking

The economy's health is anchored in **Fiat GDP** — the total USD value of real economic transactions:

- **Marketplace**: Listing fiat prices recorded as GDP on first interaction
- **Events**: Ticket prices for paid events recorded as GDP on purchase
- **Travel**: Booking fiat amounts recorded as GDP on completion

The **floor price** formula:
```
Floor Price = Total Fiat GDP (USD) ÷ Total DSP Supply
```
